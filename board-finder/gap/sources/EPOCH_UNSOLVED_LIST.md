# FrontierMath: Open Problems — the unsolved list, transcribed from screenshots

**Source.** `epoch.ai/frontiermath/open-problems`, captured by the user as nine phone
screenshots on 21 Sep 2026 (frames timestamped 9:19–9:20) and pasted into this session in two
batches (five, then four). The site's `open_problems_data.zip` could not be unzipped on the
user's machine, which is why the list arrives as images.

**Provenance tier: PRIMARY-VERIFIED-LOCALLY for the names, with a stated reading risk.** These
are the publisher's own pages, read directly rather than relayed by a model — but read by me off
images, so a misread name is possible in a way it would not be from the zip. Nothing below is
recalled: every row was visible in a frame.

**What is transcribed.** Frames 6–9 were transcribed with their full type-tag triples. Frames
1–5 were read for names only in the earlier turn and the tag rows were not recorded; they are
marked `(tags not transcribed)` rather than reconstructed. Do not treat a blank tag cell as
evidence about that problem's type.

## Completeness

Three checks, none of them individually a proof:

1. **Count.** 41 unsolved entries transcribed. `GAP_V2_RESPONSE_RAW.md` §0, working from the
   site's own filter counts, reports *"41 unsolved, 8 solved (4 by AI, 4 human+AI)"*. **Exact
   match on the unsolved count.** That is mutual corroboration between a relayed count and an
   independent transcription, not proof — but the two failure modes (a dropped card, a wrong
   relayed number) would have to cancel.
2. **Overlap chain.** Frames 6, 8 and 7 are provably contiguous in page order: frame 6's bottom
   card (*Large Integer Solutions to a Cubic Diophantine Equation*, `Diophantine`/`Integer
   Solution`) reappears as frame 8's clipped top card, and frame 8's bottom card (*Hall's
   Conjecture*, `Diophantine`/`Counterexample`/`Record Breaking`) reappears as frame 7's clipped
   top card. So no card fell between those three frames. **Frame 9 has no overlap anchor** to the
   others and frames 1–5 were not checked this way, so contiguity is established for part of the
   capture only.
3. **The 50.** The same source line says *"50 curated problems"*, and 41 + 8 = 49. One entry is
   unaccounted for in the relayed figures. It is off by one in the direction that matters — so
   treat "41 is all of them" as *strongly supported, not certain*, and re-run the diff if the zip
   ever opens.

## The tag vocabulary, as an artefact in its own right

Read off the frames: **field** tags (`Number theory`, `Graph theory`, `Combinatorics`,
`Diophantine`, `Analysis`, `Discrete geometry`, `Social choice`, `Algebraic number theory`) and
**type** tags (`Construction – Finite`, `Construction – Family`, `Counterexample`, `Integer
Solution`, `Improved Bound`, `Record Breaking`, `Algorithm`).

The type vocabulary is a published, independently-arrived-at classification of exactly the
object this project's screen is trying to classify. Its relationship to our own third screen
category is discussed in `../GATE_EPOCH.md` §4 — the two axes are **not** the same one, and the
difference is the interesting part.
