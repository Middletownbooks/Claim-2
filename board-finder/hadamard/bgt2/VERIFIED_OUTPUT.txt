  ASSERTED: every permitted row sum a_k is ODD, at every (v, d) checked.
  Because v is odd, so (v-1)/d is even for odd d, so r is odd, so
  d(2u-(r-1)) is even, so a_k = +-1 + even is odd.

  An odd square is 1 mod 8. So a sum of b odd squares is b mod 8.

      sum_{k=1..b} a_k^2 = 4v  forces  b = 4 (mod 8).

    b=  4: b mod 8 = 4, admissible v of the 13: 13
