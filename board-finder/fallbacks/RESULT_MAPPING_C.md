| **C geometry & topology** | 22 | **4.5%** |

C came in at one record in twenty-two — **lower than B**, and nowhere near the "high" rate I
predicted. **The prediction is falsified**, and by the terms I registered, the branch that fires is
the second one: the screen is not discriminating on the metric I chose.

## 2. Why, and this is the part that transfers

The metric was wrong, and I do not get to use that to rescue the prediction.

The searcher read *"what quantity does the literature track"* generously and correctly: for a
geometry conjecture, what the literature tracks is **a growing list of classes** — *"which classes
of simple manifolds are known to be boundary rigid"*, *"the class of groups currently proven to be
good"*, *"which Fano manifolds are verified"*. Fourteen of the 22 answers are of that form. That is
a real tracked quantity and a correct answer to the question I asked.

**It is just not a quantity we can move.** A class list grows by proving a theorem about a new
class. There is no number to beat and no object to construct.

So `NONE` measured *"does the literature track anything"* when what I cared about was *"is there a
number a construction or a computation could move"*. **A proxy that does not track the target** —
which is D44's failure in a new costume: last time I left a gap between the bands, this time I
picked a band that measures the wrong axis.

## 3. The metric I should have registered, applied to all three chunks

*Records whose tracked quantity is a **number** and whose `WHAT MOVES IT` is **construction** or
**computation**:*

| | records | usable | survives reading |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| A | 31 | ~4 (238, 283, 337, 345) | **345** only — 283 closed up to polylog, 337 crowded, 238 is on erdosproblems |
| B | 26 | 3 (239, 301, 348) | **239** only — 301 is pure hardware, 348 fails Δ |
| **C** | 22 | **1** (335) | **none** — 335 is the Jones Unknot Conjecture, entry #9 on Epoch's list, excluded at step 0 |

**Under the right metric the ordering does hold, C is lowest, and the margins are tiny
everywhere.** Across 79 records in three chunks, about eight have a numeric record moved by
construction or computation, and six of those die on a second criterion.

A pleasing consistency check falls out: **335 is excluded by step 0 and would have been rejected
by the v3 screen anyway.** The public-list filter and the gap-type screen agree on the one record
in C that looked usable.

## 4. Two things in C worth noting even though neither is a target

**272, Kuznetsov cubic fourfolds — genuine landmark movement, correctly placed outside the
window.** March 2026: Katzarkov–Kontsevich–Pantev–Yu's "theory of atoms" proved *rational ⟹ has an
associated K3*, and produced **the first explicit irrational cubic fourfold ever known**. The
searcher flagged it as movement but outside the 90-day window rather than reporting it as a
resolution. That is the `STATUS TODAY` field behaving exactly as intended.

**335, Jones unknot — verified to 24 crossings** (Tuzun–Sikora 2020, extending 23). A clean
computational record with a clean deliverable, unmoved for six years. It is on Epoch's list, so it
is out; but it is the only thing in 22 geometry records that had the right shape at all.
