What this cannot show
======================================================================
  `significance` is VibeMathed's own curated score, assigned by the site after
  the fact. A rising trend is consistent with a real difficulty ramp AND with
  the site's own inclusion policy tightening, or with scorer drift. Nothing here
  separates those. The trend is evidence that the corpus BEHAVES like a
  leaderboard; it is not independent measurement of model capability.

  M3 also does not compete with M1 and M2 -- it supplies the reason a short list
  of problems is shared in the first place, and the reason two labs are pushed
  into one difficulty band at one time without anyone talking to anyone.
  PASS  the confound is recorded rather than argued away
