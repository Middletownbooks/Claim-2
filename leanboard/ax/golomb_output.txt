    Hadamard    'does a matrix of order n exist?'   -> yes, decomposed

    Riemann     'every nontrivial zero...'          -> NO

  Famous problems of the first kind are exactly the ones this strategy
  reaches. Fame was never the obstruction; QUANTIFIER STRUCTURE is.

5. A third deliverable category, and the strategic consequence
============================================================================
  I  EXISTENCE WITNESS    claim coextensive with a check
     Hadamard matrix at order n; a counterexample; a Golomb ruler
     -> 100% verifiable, cheap, no provenance needed

  II RECORD / OPTIMALITY  witness is checkable, optimality is not
     an OPTIMAL Golomb ruler; a best-known bound
     -> the witness half is category I; the optimality half is category III

  III UNIVERSAL THEOREM   no finite witness exists
      CDC, Rota's basis conjecture
      -> 0% reachable by this strategy at any budget

  THE CONSEQUENCE FOR A CONSTRAINED BUDGET: category II SPLITS, and you may
  ship the half you can afford. A ruler shorter than the best known is a
  category I deliverable and a genuine result; you need not prove it
  optimal. The SSUF ladder in the corpus is exactly this pattern -- each of
  C >= 1.17353 and C >= 1.28249 is a record WITNESSED BY AN INSTANCE, with
  no optimality claimed. Nobody had to close the problem to publish.
  PASS  category II is separable, which is the affordable move
