=== Is the team's directional forecast informative about the outcome? ===
  2x2  GO/effect=1  GO/no-effect=8  NOGO/effect=2  NOGO/no-effect=10
  P(effect | GO)   = 1/9 = 0.111
  P(effect | NOGO) = 2/12 = 0.167
  base rate P(effect) = 3/21 = 0.143
  Fisher exact two-sided p = 1.0000

  -> GO predicts an effect LESS often than NOGO does. The forecast is
     not informative; it is very slightly anti-informative, and far
     from significant.

=== What the earlier framing got wrong ===
  Comparing 1/9 (precision of GO) with 10/12 (precision of NOGO) compares
  the precisions of DIFFERENT propositions. With a base rate of true
  effects near 0.14, a useless forecaster already scores ~0.86 on nulls.
  The apparent 11%% vs 83%% asymmetry is a base-rate artifact.
