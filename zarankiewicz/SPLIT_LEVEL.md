# Why the split moved from level 4 to level 5

## The symptom

Level-4 shards drained to a single thread. Shard 0 used **1.88 of 4 cores**; shard 3 was at **1.00
after forty minutes** — half its wall time on one prefix with three cores idle. With 82,512 level-4
prefixes and 37× subtree skew, every shard ends with a tail that cannot be split at prefix
granularity, however many shards there are: **the tail is one prefix.**

## The fix, and it is gated

Split at level 5 instead: **6,655,827 prefixes against 82,512**, eighty times finer, generated by
the C itself in 3.3 s into a 111 MB file.

The restructure is gated against work already done. The level-5 prefixes descending from level-4
shard 0 reproduce it exactly:

| level | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 11 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L5 units | 2,572 | 142,988 | 2,156,625 | 32,307,810 | 116,717,963 | 1,403,542,203 | 54,109,698 |
| L4 shard | 2,572 | 142,988 | 2,156,625 | 32,307,810 | 116,717,963 | 1,403,542,203 | 54,109,698 |

Leaves identical at 54,109,698; antisum histogram identical at `{221: 4,488, 223: 54,105,210}`.

## The same work, three ways — attributing the gain honestly

| configuration | wall s | core s | cores used | wall vs first |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| level-4 split, **all** survivors written | 945.0 | 1,774.7 | 1.88 of 4 | 1.00× |
| level-4 split, `-survmax 221` | 775.7 | 1,297.1 | 1.67 of 4 | 1.22× |
| **level-5 split, `-survmax 221`** | **344.4** | 1,321.7 | **3.84 of 4** | **2.74×** |

* **the survivor I/O fix**: 27% of core-seconds, **1.22×** of wall;
* **the finer split**: cores used 1.67 → 3.84, a 2.29× parallelism gain, **2.25×** of wall on top;
* together **2.74×**.

Two things this corrects. The gain is **two fixes, not one** — and I had claimed the I/O was costing
**45% of throughput** when it is 27% of core-seconds and 1.22× of wall. Measuring the same work
three ways rather than inferring from one comparison is the only reason either number is
trustworthy, and conflating configurations is the error this programme has recorded four times
today under `D68`.

## What is kept

The three completed level-4 shards stay in `sweep/` as a record and as the cross-check above. The
level-5 sweep writes to `sweep_L5/`. They must not be pooled — they cover overlapping work.
